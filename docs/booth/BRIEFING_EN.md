# Rai Mai Phao (Zero-Burn Farm) — Team Briefing

*Simple English (A2–B2). All prices and numbers are **our best guess** for the prototype. We still need to test a real machine.*

---

## 1. The project in a few words
Farmers burn crop waste because it is **the cheapest way** to clean the field. We want to make crop waste **worth money**.
Our machine **KhumKha** ("worth it" in Thai) sits in a **village station**. It turns crop waste into **animal food, fuel blocks or compost**.
Farmers sell with **LINE**. A **driver from the village** picks up the waste from the farm, like Grab.
**Farmers get money, drivers get work, buyers get cheap material, and everyone gets cleaner air.**

## 3. How it works (5 steps)
1. **Ask in LINE:** the farmer says what waste they have and how much. They bring it, or ask for a pickup.
2. **Village pickup:** a driver from the village takes the job and picks it up from the farm.
3. **Check at the station:** we weigh it, take a photo and check if it's dry. We scan the farmer's QR code.
4. **Make the product:** A, B or C.
5. **The farmer chooses:** sell it to us, let us sell it for them, or take it home. They get a receipt in LINE.

**The station has 8 parts:** scale for trucks · drying area with a roof · machine room · solar roof · storage · shop and office · fire safety · parking. We start with **one machine in each village** and add more later.

## 4. How the machine works (top to bottom)
| Height | Part | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| 140–180 cm | opening with a safety lid, hand guard, magnet, wet-meter, touch screen, QR scanner, camera | takes in the waste and checks it |
| 100–140 cm | cutter + 2 screens | cuts the waste into small pieces |
| 85–100 cm | moving gate | sends it to A, B or C |
| 30–85 cm | A pellet press · C straight path · B hot press + cutter | makes the product |
| 0–30 cm | 3 trays with scales | weighs the product |
| Back | dust fan and bag, power box, air holes | keeps it safe, clean and cool |

**Limits:** it makes one product at a time (we use a queue) · when it changes from fuel to animal food, it must clean itself first · if the cutter breaks, the machine stops (we keep extra blades, and the system calls a technician).

## 5. Business model (BMC)
| Part | What we have |
|---|---|
| Customers | farmers who sell waste · people who buy our 6 products · drivers |
| What we give them | farmers: money and pickup from the farm · buyers: material every month that they can check · village: clean air |
| How we reach them | LINE · the drying area in the village · village leaders |
| Relationship | LINE · clear receipts · a public dashboard |
| How we make money | **selling products (most money)** · a fee if farmers take products home · 10% of each pickup fee · later: company sponsors, local government, carbon credits |
| Main work | buy, make, sell · run LINE · take care of the machines |
| What we need | the machine · LINE system and data · drivers · our brand |
| Partners | village groups · local government · the university · mushroom farms, cow farms, longan dryers · drivers |
| Costs | machine (about 200,000 baht) · electricity (less with solar) · buying waste · repairs · workers · pickup fund |

**Who gets something:** farmers (money) · village drivers (extra money) · buyers (cheap material every month) · the village (less smoke, fewer sick people) · local government (a way to stop burning, with real data) · nature (less carbon, waste goes back to the soil).
**People, Planet, Profit:** People = money for farmers and drivers · Planet = less smoke and carbon · Profit = selling products and fees.

## 8. Questions and easy answers

### The problem and the idea
- **Why do farmers burn?** It's the fastest and cheapest way to clean the field. Ploughing costs money and gives nothing back.
- **Why not stop burning with a law?** There are laws, but farmers still burn if there is no cheaper way. We give them money, so they have a reason to stop.
- **Who are your customers?** Two groups: farmers who sell waste, and people who buy our products (mushroom farms, cow farms, animal farms, factories that need fuel).

### The machine
- **What is the machine called?** KhumKha. It means "worth it" in Thai.
- **What does it make?** A: animal food or fuel pellets. B: fuel blocks. C: compost or material for mushrooms.
- **How fast is it?** About 15 minutes for 100 kg of C, 60 minutes for A, 75 minutes for B.
- **What if the waste is wet?** If it's more than 20% wet, we can't take it. The farmer can dry it at the station.
- **What if it breaks?** We keep extra blades. The system calls a technician every 200 hours.
- **Is it safe?** Yes. It has a safety lid, a hand guard, a magnet for metal, a red stop button and a dust fan.
- **Did you build it?** We made a full 3D design and working software. Next, we want to build a real one.

### Money
- **How do you make money?** We sell the products. Farmers pay a small fee to take products home. We keep 10% of each pickup fee.
- **How much is the machine?** About 200,000 baht (150,000–280,000). The station cost depends on the village.
- **When do you get the money back?** In about 17 months, if we use 400 kg a day and make about 1.2 baht per kg. This is a guess.
- **Why 1 baht a kilo?** It's better than burning (0 baht), and we still make a profit.
- **Who pays the pickup fund?** It comes from our product sales. It's only for farmers who sell to us.
- **Why 30%?** So the pickup fee doesn't take most of a small farmer's money.
- **Can people ask for very small pickups?** No. You need 300 kg or a full truck. We tell small farms to share a trip.
- **Who are your competitors?** Big power plants buy crop waste, but only near the plant and in big amounts. We go to small farmers in the village.

### Impact, technology and law
- **Which SDGs?** 3, 8, 12, 13 (also 7 and 15).
- **How do you measure your impact?** Every job saves the data: kg not burned, less PM2.5 (about 8 g/kg), money for farmers and drivers. You can see it on the dashboard.
- **Is 8 g/kg a real number?** It's our guess. We will check it with research.
- **Why LINE?** Farmers already use LINE every day. They don't need a new app.
- **What about personal data?** Farmers say yes first. We keep only the data we need. We hide names in public. Farmers can delete their data.
- **Can you sell the animal food?** First, we use it on farms in the village. To sell it, we need a permit.
- **Can you protect your brand?** Yes. We can register "Rai Mai Phao" as a trademark.

### Hard questions
- **What is your biggest risk?** We haven't tested the numbers yet. Next, we want to build one real machine.
- **What would you do with money?** Build one machine in a village in Chiang Rai, test it, and fix our numbers.
- **What did you learn?** The problem is not that farmers don't know. The problem is money. So we make the good choice the cheaper choice.
